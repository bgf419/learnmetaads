How the lessons work

Each lesson is a short run of steps. Every step makes one point and shows it moving, and most let you drag, tap or replay. Read them in order: each step builds on the one before it, and each lesson on the last.

Every number tells you where it came from:

Our dataOur own studies of the public Meta Ad Library.

Meta saysMeta’s own help pages, engineering posts or earnings calls.

ReportedWhat media buyers widely report but Meta hasn’t confirmed.

ExampleMade-up numbers that keep the arithmetic easy to follow.
